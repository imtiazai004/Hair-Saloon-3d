Good to Know

Where is Sinma Hair And Beauty?

On Garratt Lane in Tooting, London SW17 0LN. Open directions in Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any Book button or ask our assistant. Your request opens in WhatsApp ready to send, and the salon confirms your time there. You can also call +44 7311 189118.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on your hair and the treatment. Tap “Ask price” next to any service on the services page for a personal quote on WhatsApp.

Are you open on Sundays?

No, the salon is closed on Sundays, but you can book online for Monday to Saturday at any time.
